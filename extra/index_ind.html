01. About

Research Background

I am a PhD graduate from the Faculty of Information Technology and Bionics, Pázmány Péter Catholic University (PPKE ITK), Budapest, Hungary (2017–2023), supervised by Prof. Csaba Benedek at the Machine Perception Laboratory (MPLab), HUN-REN SZTAKI, where I have conducted six years of research. My doctoral dissertation, "Deep Learning Based Methods on 2D Image and 3D Structure Inpainting", addresses the intersection of computer vision, deep generative modeling, and 3D scene understanding. I hold a Master's degree (2013–2016) and Bachelor's degree (2009–2013) from Tishreen University, Latakia, Syria, with theses focused on 3D environment reconstruction using RGB-D cameras and laser-based object tracking.

My primary research contributions span multi-view 3D point cloud completion for autonomous driving and robotic perception (MVPCC-Net, Elsevier IVC 2023), GAN-based blind inpainting of masonry and architectural structures (WACV 2021, MDPI Remote Sensing 2020), and semantic segmentation of multispectral satellite imagery for inland excess water detection (ICCVW 2025, IEEE/CVF). Additional collaborations have contributed to brain-computer interfaces via EEG-based motor imagery classification (IEEE BCI 2020, 57 citations).

I am actively engaged in drone perception and underwater LiDAR sensing through the EU Proactif project, and am exploring Neural Radiance Fields (NeRF) and 3D Gaussian Splatting for real-time scene reconstruction. I am seeking research scientist positions where I can advance the frontiers of 3D vision and generative modeling.

02. Research

Research Areas

My work sits at the intersection of 3D computer vision, deep generative modeling, and geospatial remote sensing — with a focus on scene reconstruction, structural inpainting, and intelligent satellite image analysis.

3D Scene Reconstruction

Multi-view deep learning for LiDAR point cloud completion in autonomous driving and robotic perception contexts. Novel view synthesis and real-time rendering for complex scenes.

NeRF 3D Gaussian Splatting Point Cloud Multi-View

Image & Structure Inpainting

GAN-based blind inpainting of 2D textures and 3D structures, with applications to heritage masonry restoration, damage detection, and urban scene completion from LiDAR data.

GAN Blind Inpainting Generative Models

Deep Learning Architectures

Design and training of CNN, GAN, Transformer, and Autoregressive architectures for semantic segmentation, object recognition, generative image synthesis, and 3D shape generation.

Transformer Autoregressive CNN GAN

Remote Sensing & Geospatial AI

Multispectral and SAR image fusion from Sentinel-1/2 satellites. Temporal gap-filling, semantic segmentation for change detection, and large-scale inland flood monitoring.

Sentinel-1/2 SAR Fusion Segmentation Time Series

Research Projects & Demonstrations

3D Vision

3D Point Cloud Completion for Robotic Perception

MVPCC-Net · Image and Vision Computing, Elsevier · 2023 · 11 citations

Addresses the challenge of incomplete LiDAR scans caused by occlusion in autonomous driving and mobile robotics. MVPCC-Net aggregates multi-view 2D depth projections through a deep encoder-decoder architecture, producing geometrically accurate and dense 3D point cloud completions for occluded vehicles and objects.

Method: Multi-view 2D projection · Graph-based feature aggregation · PointNet++ encoder · Folding-based decoder · Trained on ShapeNet & SZTAKI MLS dataset

Remote Sensing

Inland Excess Water Monitoring via Satellite Imagery

SplitClass · ICCVW, IEEE/CVF SEA Workshop · 2025

Develops a temporal-aware semantic segmentation framework for detecting and mapping inland excess water (IEW) using multi-temporal Sentinel-1 SAR and Sentinel-2 multispectral imagery. A temporal gap-filling module handles missing observations due to cloud contamination, enabling continuous operational monitoring across agricultural regions.

Method: SplitClass segmentation network · Sentinel-1/2 fusion · Temporal gap-filling · Deployed on HuggingFace Spaces

Image Analysis

GAN-Based Masonry Wall Analysis & Structural Inpainting

WACV 2021 · MDPI Remote Sensing 2020 · 39 citations

Develops a full pipeline for automated analysis of historical masonry walls: CNN-based watershed-driven brick segmentation, followed by a GAN-based blind inpainting model that reconstructs damaged or missing regions without requiring explicit damage masks. Evaluated on a custom dataset of Hungarian heritage structures.

Method: Watershed marker extraction (CNN) · Blind inpainting GAN · No mask supervision · Heritage wall dataset · Live interactive demo
