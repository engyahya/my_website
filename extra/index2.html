About

I am a PhD student and Research Associate specializing in deep learning-based machine perception technologies. My work focuses on advancing the field of computer vision through research and implementation of state-of-the-art algorithms for visual and LiDAR data processing.

Currently pursuing my doctorate at Pázmány Péter Catholic University's Roska Tamás Doctoral School in collaboration with HUN-REN SZTAKI - Institute for Computer Science and Control. My research interests include 3D human pose estimation, person re-identification, and robotics with reinforcement learning. I hold an MSc in Image Processing and Computer Vision from the Erasmus Mundus Joint Masters program with excellence scholarship.
