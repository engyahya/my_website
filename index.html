01. About Me

About

I am a PhD graduate from the Faculty of Information Technology and Bionics, Pázmány Péter Catholic University (PPKE ITK), Budapest, Hungary (2017–2023), supervised by Prof. Csaba Benedek at the Machine Perception Laboratory (MPLab), HUN-REN SZTAKI, where I have six years of research experience. My doctoral dissertation, "Deep Learning Based Methods on 2D Image and 3D Structure Inpainting", spans the intersection of computer vision, deep learning, and 3D scene understanding. I hold a Master's degree (2013–2016) and Bachelor's degree (2009–2013) from Tishreen University, Latakia, Syria, where my theses focused on 3D environment reconstruction using RGB-D cameras and laser-based object tracking.

My core research revolves around 3D point cloud completion for vehicles and urban scenes, GAN-based image inpainting for masonry and architectural structures, and remote sensing using Sentinel-1/2 imagery. I collaborate closely with researchers at HUN-REN SZTAKI and have contributed to projects in brain-computer interfaces (EEG classification), pattern recognition, and geospatial AI.

02. Research

Research Interests

My work sits at the intersection of 3D computer vision, deep learning, and remote sensing — with a focus on scene reconstruction, inpainting, and intelligent image analysis.

3D Scene Reconstruction

Multi-view point cloud completion, Neural Radiance Fields (NeRF), 3D Gaussian Splatting, and Autoregressive 3D models for 3D shape generation.

Image & Scene Inpainting

GAN-based blind inpainting of 2D images and 3D structures, with applications to masonry wall restoration, and urban scene reconstruction from Lidar data.

Deep Learning for Vision

CNN, GAN, Transformer, and autoregressive architectures for semantic segmentation, object recognition, generative image synthesis and generative 3D modeling.

Remote Sensing & Geospatial AI

Sentinel-1/2 multispectral imagery, Time series analysis, and change detection.
